AI / NLP / CONVERSATIONAL APPLICATION

Multilingual Cooperative Governance & Legal Assistance Chatbot

A chatbot project focused on multilingual assistance for cooperative governance and legal information. The concept is to make relevant information easier to access through a conversational interface and help users navigate governance-related questions across language barriers.

THE PROBLEM

People may encounter language barriers or difficulty navigating governance-related information and legal resources. A conversational interface can help make information easier to find and understand.

THE SOLUTION

A multilingual assistance interface designed to support users seeking cooperative governance and legal information.

KEY OBJECTIVES

  • Improve accessibility to information across supported languages.
  • Make governance-related information easier to explore.
  • Provide a user-friendly conversational experience.
  • Help users identify relevant legal or governance resources.
